What is Temp Chat?

Temp Chat is a highly secure, anonymous P2P chat room designed for absolute privacy. Unlike traditional messaging applications, Temp Chat operates entirely via WebRTC architecture, creating a direct serverless connection between you and your chat partner.

Unbreakable End-to-End Encryption

Your digital security is our utmost priority. Every single message sent through Temp Chat is protected by military-grade AES-GCM 256-bit End-to-End Encryption. The decryption keys are generated locally on your browser and are never transmitted to any central server. This means no one, not even us, can read your conversations.

Strict No Logs Policy

True to our name, this is a strict no log chat platform. We do not store your IP addresses, we do not save your message history, and there are absolutely no databases involved in the chat process. The moment you close your browser tab, your chat room and all its contents are permanently and irreversibly destroyed.

How to Use Our Serverless Chat?

Getting started is instant and completely free. Simply click the "Create Chat Room" button at the top of this page. A unique, encrypted link will be generated for you. Share this link with anyone you want to communicate with. There are no registrations, no accounts to create, and no application downloads required. Just pure, private communication.
